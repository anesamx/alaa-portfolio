# Front-end and UI/UX

This document outlines the design, structure, and user experience for Alaa's portfolio website.

## **Global-Styling**

*   **Cursor:** The cursor will be styled as a paintbrush. As it moves, it will leave a subtle, multi-colored trail, which will fade out.
*   **Background:** The background will be a clean, off-white, textured like a canvas. A subtle, blurred layer will sit behind the main content to create a sense of depth and an artistic, authentic feel.

## Hero Section

*   **Layout:** A full-screen, centered layout to immediately focus the user's attention.
*   **Image:** A circular, high-quality image of the artist, Alaa.
*   **Typography:**
    *   The name "Alaa" will be displayed prominently below the image in an elegant, large serif font.
    *   Her username, "application art," will appear beneath her name in a smaller, clean sans-serif font.
*   **Call-to-Action Buttons:**
    *   Three buttons will be arranged horizontally below the username: "My Work", "Contact Me", and "Support Me".
    *   These buttons will have a modern design with a subtle glow effect on hover, inviting interaction.

## About Me Section

*   **Background:** This section will feature an off-white, textured background to create a cozy and inviting atmosphere.
*   **Text:** The text will have a "raised" effect, achieved with a subtle shadow to make it stand out. 
*   **Animation:** To enhance the artistic feel, emojis of brushes, palettes, and sparkles (🎨, 🖌️, ✨) will gently float and drift across the text, creating a magical and engaging experience.

## Gallery Section

*   **Random Work Button:** Before the main gallery, a button labeled "Random Work" will be featured. Clicking it will open a modal and display a single, randomly selected piece of art.
*   **Layout:** The gallery will consist of three horizontal rows of images.
*   **Animation:** Each row will continuously slide horizontally at a different, random speed, creating a dynamic and visually interesting parallax effect.
*   **Image Styling:**
    *   Images will be randomly selected from the collection.
    *   Each image will have rounded corners and a distinct, randomly colored border to add a vibrant, scrapbook-like feel.
*   **Interactivity:** When a user clicks on any image, a modal window will open, displaying a larger, high-resolution version of the artwork, allowing for detailed viewing.

## Thank You Section

*   **Text:** A final, bold "Thanks for visiting!" message.
*   **Border:** The text container will have a border that slowly animates through a spectrum of colors.
*   **Animation:** A subtle, shining "brushstroke" animation will periodically sweep across the text, giving it a magical, glistening effect.
